# Complaints, service levels and outages

Complaints are resolved within a service level that depends on priority: critical issues such as a power outage
within 4 hours, high-priority voltage or meter faults within 24 hours, billing and connection questions within 48
hours, and other requests within 72 hours. Customers can follow each ticket, reply to the support team, and rate the
resolution.

Planned maintenance and unplanned outages are published for the affected pincodes with an estimated restoration time.
Customers in those areas receive a notification when an outage is declared and when power is restored.
