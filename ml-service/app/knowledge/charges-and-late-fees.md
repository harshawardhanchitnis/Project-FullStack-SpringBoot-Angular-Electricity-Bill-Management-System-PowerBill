# Fixed charges, FAC, duty and late payment surcharge

Every bill includes a fixed charge based on the sanctioned load in kW, a fuel adjustment charge (FAC) per billable kWh
that passes through changes in power purchase cost, and electricity duty levied by the state government as a
percentage of the charges. Electricity supply is generally exempt from GST.

Bills are due fifteen days after issue. After a short grace period, a late payment surcharge accrues daily on the
unpaid principal and is capped at a fixed percentage of the principal. Paying several bills together settles each
invoice and its surcharge in one atomic transaction, and a receipt is issued once the gateway confirms payment.
