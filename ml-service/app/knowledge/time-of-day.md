# Time-of-day (ToD) tariffs

Under time-of-day pricing the energy rate depends on when electricity is used. GridIQ tariffs define three windows:

- Solar hours (09:00-17:00): a rebate on the slab rate, because rooftop and utility solar make daytime energy cheap.
- Evening peak (18:00-22:00): a surcharge on the slab rate, because demand on the grid is highest.
- Normal hours (all other times): the slab rate applies unchanged.

Shifting flexible loads such as washing machines, water heaters, pumps and electric vehicle charging from the evening
peak to solar hours lowers the ToD adjustment on the bill without reducing total consumption.
