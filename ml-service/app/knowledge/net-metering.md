# Rooftop solar net metering

Prosumers with rooftop solar use a bi-directional smart meter that records imported and exported energy separately.
Under kWh net metering, exported units first offset imported units in the same billing cycle. If exports exceed
imports, the surplus is carried forward as a kWh credit to the next cycle. Some tariffs instead settle the surplus as
a cash credit at the solar export rate. Fixed charges and electricity duty still apply even when net consumption is
zero. Schemes such as PM Surya Ghar subsidise residential rooftop systems.
